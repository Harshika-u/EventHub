About JGS - ISC

An education measured in more than results.

Johnson Grammar School was recently ranked 2nd in Telangana for ISC 2026 results — a reflection of a campus where academics and student-led activity have always grown side by side. The ISC curriculum is designed to improve oral and writing skills, along with in-depth learning of various concepts, which focuses on the overall development of students. A critical two-year “stream-based”, subject-specific, competency-based teaching and learning and assessment programme which is offered by the Council for the Indian School Certificate Examination. The ISC curriculum places a lot of importance on English literature & language along with elective subjects. It is very systematic; disciplined & the difficulty level of the subjects is equally distributed in exams. At JGS, the ISC curriculum is offered to students of classes XI & XII with English as the medium of instruction in the following streams,
SCIENCE- M.P.C, Bi.P.C, M.Bi.P.C
COMMERCE- M.A.E, A.E.C
